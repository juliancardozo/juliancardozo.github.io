How I lead

Four principles I bring to every team

Context over micromanagement

A team decides well when it understands the problem and its constraints. My job is to create that context, not to make every decision.

Teams that don't depend on heroes

If something only works when one person is around, it isn't solved yet. I aim for shared knowledge and sustainable delivery.

Architecture follows the business

Multi-tenancy, dual currency or an event ledger are business decisions written in code, and they're expensive if they arrive late.

AI amplifies judgment; it does not replace it

It speeds up researching, specifying and building. Deciding what gets built, reviewing what it produces and owning the result stays with people.
