Privacy

Newsletter privacy policy

I use subscription data only to send the newsletter and updates related to the content, projects and professional conversations I publish on this site.

What data is stored

When you subscribe, your email and the main interest you selected in the form are recorded. If you fill in your name, it is stored too, to personalize future communications.

Minimal context metadata is also recorded: the page you subscribed from, the type of form and the time of consent.

What it is used for

The data is used to send you the newsletter, understand which topics draw the most interest and organize future conversations or editorial segments.

I do not sell or share your information with third parties. Subscriptions are processed using Resend as the email infrastructure provider.

Consent and unsubscribing

Subscribing requires explicit consent in the form. If at some point you no longer want to receive emails, you will be able to unsubscribe from the email itself or by writing to me directly.

Until the automatic unsubscribe flow is live in production, you can also ask for your data to be deleted or updated by replying to any message or writing to my professional email.

Security and contact

The Resend API key is not exposed on this website. Subscriptions are processed through a secure endpoint outside GitHub Pages.

If you need to access, correct or delete your data, you can write to me from the contact section of the site.
